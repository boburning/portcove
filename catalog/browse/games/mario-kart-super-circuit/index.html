Setup

  1. Keep the complete player together

    Use the complete Windows x64 ZIP with MarioKartSuperCircuitRecomp.exe, libraries, assets, mods and overlay_toolchain together; no separate game installer is required. On Linux, mark the official x64 AppImage executable. Its launcher keeps writable settings and mods in the per-user data home, normally ~/.local/share/MarioKartSuperCircuitRecomp, rather than beside the AppImage. Upstream’s Linux guide covers FUSE 2 and the supported --appimage-extract-and-run fallback.

  2. Choose Play, then select any missing original files

    Add the player in Portcove, then choose Play to open the upstream launcher. Supply your own matching USA Mario Kart: Super Circuit GBA game and retail GBA BIOS when requested. The launcher validates those inputs and remembers valid choices. Portcove discovery and registration do not run the bundled compilers or helpers.

Setup notes
Keep your actual saves and settings
Normal .sav files and optional .stateN slots follow your selected original game file, unless you chose custom save/state paths. Preserve that actual folder and its .tmp, .bak and .prev-size.bak recovery siblings separately; the launcher save-path display does not relocate saves. On Windows, also keep local config.ini, keybinds.ini, rom.cfg, bios.cfg, user game.toml, any selected local saves/ and the complete mods/ tree. On Linux, preserve those settings and mod choices from $XDG_DATA_HOME/MarioKartSuperCircuitRecomp, normally ~/.local/share/MarioKartSuperCircuitRecomp.
Prepare updates beside your working copy
You or upstream update this registered runtime. On Windows, retain the working folder and prepare the new version separately; do not carry generated recomp_cache or diagnostics into a new runtime. On Linux, different AppImage files normally share the same data home, including caches, and refresh executable/assets links there at startup. Back up that actual home and your ROM-adjacent saves before trying another AppImage; a separate AppImage file alone does not isolate your data. Upstream has changed bundled mod contents without changing their package version, so preserve both catalogs and reconcile choices deliberately rather than overwriting them. Portcove registration offers no managed Update, Backup or Rollback and removing it leaves every external file untouched.
