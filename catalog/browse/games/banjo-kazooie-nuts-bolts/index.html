Setup

  1. Use permitted existing copies and local preparation

    Keep permitted copies of reNut and NutStaller, and point NutStaller at your player folder. If extract-xiso is missing, its separate Get extract-xiso action downloads that tool. Then use 3. Pick ISO + extract with your own USA Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts Xbox 360 ISO and 4. Add title update with your matching original update as required by the current release instructions. Back up the prepared folder before approving its replacement. These local preparation actions do not acquire another runtime; Get latest reNut and Do everything do and require separate acquisition permission. Portcove does not run the tools during discovery.

  2. Add the prepared player, then choose Play

    On Windows select the prepared renut.exe; on Linux select the prepared renut.AppImage and its matching folder. Save the launch details, then choose Play separately. If preparing the player manually, its first-run path wizard asks for existing game-data, user-data and update folders and remembers those paths. No game, installer or helper is executed merely by discovering or registering the copy.

Setup notes
Keep the richer upstream launcher available
NutStaller provides runtime update checks, controls, Workshop and save tools; keep it available after registering the prepared player. On Linux, its current instructions require glibc 2.17 for NutStaller itself, 2.34 for the extractor and 2.38 for the player. The player and preparation helper have separate requirements.
Preserve actual saves, settings and custom content
Close the game before backup or replacement. Keep your actual selected user-data root; defaults are Documents/renut on Windows and XDG_DATA_HOME/renut, normally ~/.local/share/renut, on Linux. Keep renut.cfg and renut.toml beside the Windows player or in Linux user data, plus the original ISO/update, prepared game and DLC, mods, Workshop content and nutstaller.ini beside the helper. Beta12 also documents custom music in music/ beside the Windows player or ~/.local/share/renut/music on Linux; preserve that folder separately from any custom save path.
Keep update and removal ownership clear
You or upstream update this runtime. Preserve the working copy and actual data before accepting an upstream replacement; a save-tool backup does not establish that every separate mod, music, source or helper folder was included. Portcove registration offers no managed Update, Backup or Rollback and does not adopt these files. Removing the registration preserves every external file.
