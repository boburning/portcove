Super Mario Maker 2 (smm2recomp)

Super Mario Maker 2 uses a launcher that builds your own game and 3.0.3 update locally. Packages are available for Windows, Linux and Apple silicon Mac. The project reports course play on Mac and tester results on Windows. Steam Deck performance is slow.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Provide your base game and 3.0.3 update as NSP or XCI files, or extracted ExeFS and RomFS. Setup also needs prod.keys and firmware system NCAs for fonts and Miis. The optional 100 Mario Challenge needs your own Wii U Super Mario Maker dump.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Choose a launcher for Windows x64 x86-64-v2, Linux x64 v2 or aarch64, or Apple silicon macOS 15 or later. Intel Mac on macOS 15 or later with AVX2, Android and iOS use separate source builds.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
Linux ARM reached the title in a virtual machine. Android evidence comes from emulator tests. The project has not tested Nextendo online on physical hardware.
