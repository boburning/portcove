Setup

  1. Prepare your original game

    For the managed direct-player route, copy your own original PC Sheep, Dog ’n’ Wolf game data into game-data beside SheepD3D.exe on Windows or SheepD3D on Linux, with Levels directly inside game-data. Keep the original executable and DLLs in the source copy. The maintained --game-dir=game-data argument selects that folder; the game reads its saved rendering/input options. No engine compilation is needed. For external registration, supply your actual --game-dir=PATH in Change launch details, or use upstream’s SheepLauncher.exe workflow with its own installation.
