Setup

  1. Keep the complete portable player

    The inspected Windows ZIP and Linux x86-64 TAR.XZ contain tails-adventure.exe or tails-adventure and its assets tree; no original ROM, BIOS, first-run extraction or compilation is required by this route. Keep matching assets/default_config and packaged resources.

  2. Keep your saves, options and mods

    The Windows and Linux players store both options and saved progress in config beside the executable. Supported mods live in mods/ beside it. Keep both with their creating runtime and close the game normally before maintenance: the inspected title writes config during shutdown. Put replacement content in the supported mods tree; direct edits to shipped assets are outside the declared preserved data and can block update or removal until preserved and reconciled. Runtime rollback retains current data and does not rewind saves or guarantee cross-version mod/save compatibility.

  3. Use the upstream Android app

    Install Tails Adventure by mechakotik from Google Play or choose the publisher’s Android APK on GitHub Releases. The inspected APK requires Android 5.0 or later and OpenGL ES 2.0. Keep using the same distribution channel for updates so your app signature stays compatible.

  4. Keep the Android save and mods

    With the game closed, preserve config and mods/ from the app’s writable files folder when your device gives you access, normally Android/data/com.mechakotik.tailsadventure/files. If the game cannot use external app storage, it saves config in private app storage. Check your Android backup before clearing app data, uninstalling or changing channels.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream reports missing nonessential original features; completion/gameplay claims belong to upstream and have not been repeated here. Preserve a separate copy before changing mods or data.
