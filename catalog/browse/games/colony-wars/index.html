Setup

  1. Use the exact original files

    Use your own Colony Wars (USA) two-disc set (SLUS-00543, SLUS-00554), each disc with its data track and its audio track, and your own retail SCPH-1001 BIOS. Portcove checks the files and builds the player.

Limitations and evidence

  • Portcove checks each disc’s data track and track count. Keep every disc intact, with both of its tracks.
  • Native build, disc switching, gameplay and save compatibility remain unobserved.
