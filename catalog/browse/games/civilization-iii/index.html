Setup

  1. Keep a permitted player and its companions

    For a permitted Windows copy, select OpenCiv3.exe with its folder as the working directory. Keep the complete extracted player folder, including OpenCiv3.pck, data_C7_windows_x86_64, Assets, Text and Lua.

  2. Keep the complete Linux or Mac player

    For a permitted Linux x86-64 copy, unpack the ZIP and its inner TGZ, then run OpenCiv3.x86_64 from the complete extracted folder. On Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, unpack both ZIP layers and open OpenCiv3.app through macOS. Keep the adjacent Assets, Text and Lua with the app. These are upstream setup instructions for a permitted copy.

  3. Choose game content on first Play

    The early pre-alpha player can use your own Civilization III Conquests or Complete files. Select your original game folder in the player, or choose standalone mode only when you have permission for its bundled placeholder assets. The original Civilization III executable does not need to run.

Setup notes
Preserve your working copy and saves
You or upstream provide runtime updates. Preserve the complete working copy and your chosen save locations before replacing it, including C7.ini and custom Text, Assets or Lua content. The save dialog can write to your original Civ3 folder. Portcove saves launch details without adopting or deleting external files and offers no managed backup or runtime rollback for this copy.
