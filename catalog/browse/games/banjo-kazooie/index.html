Setup

  1. Finish setup in the upstream Android app

    Install the Android ARM64 APK from this fork’s selected release, then open the app and choose your own North American 1.0 Banjo-Kazooie ROM when asked. The app handles its supported ROM import; there is no PC extraction/build step for the player. Desktop Banjo releases and another fork’s setup are separate choices.

  2. Keep your data when changing versions

    Saves, configuration, imported ROM, mods, texture packs and caches use app-private storage under com.aure.banjorecomp. Preserve that data before uninstalling or clearing app data. Follow the upstream Android update process so the installed app identity/signature can be retained. Portcove does not back up this Android profile or manage APK replacement.

Setup

  1. Finish setup in the upstream Android app

    Install this fork’s Android APK and open it to generate the Banjo-Kazooie O2R on the device. No PC or separate extraction app is needed. Supply your own supported .z64 ROM; the selected guide lists US 1.0, US 1.1, Japanese and PAL versions. US 1.0 is the recommended base if you need the fork’s ROM-hack support. Follow the app’s own import and Play flow.

  2. Keep your data when changing versions

    Keep your original ROM and back up the Android app’s actual saves, configuration, generated O2R and mods before changing or removing it. The selected release confirms on-device generation and mod/ROM-hack support, but does not establish an Android save/export path. Parent desktop paths are not an Android storage contract. Updates remain upstream-owned.
