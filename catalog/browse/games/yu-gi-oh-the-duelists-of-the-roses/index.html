Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA NTSC-U SLUS20515 ISO. Extract SLUS_205.15 and SYSTEM.CNF, then recompile the DATA/*MODULE.MRG overlays locally using the upstream instructions.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    No ready-to-use release is offered. Follow the local Linux build instructions with CMake, Ghidra and files extracted from your own disc.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream describes early movie, story, duel and map support. Its build and testing cover only the USA disc on Linux; Portcove has not confirmed gameplay.
