Pokémon: Let’s Go, Eevee! (letsgo-recomp)

Play the experimental Windows build with files from your own Let’s Go, Eevee! game. Version 1.0.0 has the most testing and enables all options. The project has not confirmed a full playthrough. Online play, a second player and Poké Ball Plus are unavailable.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Extract the Eevee edition’s ExeFS and RomFS from your own XCI or NSP before starting. The guide accepts any region. Versions 1.0.1 and 1.0.2 have fewer options and less testing.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Use the project’s Windows 10 or 11 x64 ZIP launcher. It imports and recompiles the game once. Use a separate folder for each edition. Your PC needs SSE4.2 and Vulkan 1.3, with AVX2 recommended.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
Other native desktop systems are unconfirmed. Higher frame rates, frame generation and wild battles are experimental. The extracted-file route lists no separate keys or firmware.
