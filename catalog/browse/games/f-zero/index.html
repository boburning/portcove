Setup

  1. Start with your original game

    On first Play, select your own F-Zero USA ROM and press Play in the launcher. The package includes its native runtime and does not require generating or compiling game code. The launcher remembers the selected ROM path.

  2. Set up the Apple Silicon app

    On Apple Silicon with macOS 13 or later, download the upstream arm64 ZIP and extract the complete app outside your Portcove library. Follow upstream’s first-launch instructions. Choose your own supported F-Zero USA ROM. Keep the actual FZeroRecomp profile under Library/Application Support and any custom save folder.

Setup

  1. Use a separate reviewed Windows v1.8.3 copy

    If you already have FZeroSNESRecomp-1.8.3-windows-x64.zip from the linked publisher release, extract the whole ZIP outside your Portcove library. Use a short, writable path with basic Latin characters. Open FZeroSNESRecomp.exe and select your own unmodified, headerless F-Zero (USA) .sfc file.

Setup notes
Keep your saves and settings
Keep the original game file separately. Preserve saves/ and config.ini, fzero-video.ini, keybinds.ini and rom.cfg beside the executable when moving your external Windows copy.

Limitations and evidence

  • F-Zero Forever 2.x is a separate package. Keep its runtime and profile outside your existing Portcove installation.
