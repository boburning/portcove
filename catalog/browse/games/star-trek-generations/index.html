Setup

  1. Install from both owned discs

    Open the publisher’s setup and supply Disc 1 and Disc 2 in their respective fields. Use your physical CDs, matching ISO or BIN/CUE images, or separate extracted disc folders. Choose a new destination and keep your originals separately. Setup copies about 1.1 GB and extracts the original program from Disc 1’s SUPP.Z. Open the installed game from its Start menu shortcut.

  2. Keep your local progress

    Close the game and preserve the installed game/hd folder, including SAVEGAME, plus generations.ini and generations_registry.txt beside the native player. Keep the actual STG_REGFILE location if you set an override. Back those up before replacing the runtime or running its uninstaller, which removes the installed game tree.

Limitations and evidence

  • The Windows preview uses your original disc contents and its own installer and uninstaller. Preserve both discs for future setup.
