Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (ssbu-arena)

SSBU Arena is an independent Windows fork for competitive one-on-one Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. The v0.2.52 release provides SSBU-Arena.exe to prepare and update the game from your own supported files. Upstream reported testing rollback with two windows on one PC. Portcove has yet to verify setup, matchmaking or gameplay.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (ssbu-recomp)

This experimental Windows port of Super Smash Bros. Ultimate builds locally from your own 13.0.4 or 13.0.5 game. The project reports a scripted CPU match and one Linux match. Physical controllers and matches with three or more players remain unconfirmed.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (WindowSmash)

WindowSmash builds Super Smash Bros. Ultimate 13.0.5 from your own Switch game. The project reports matches on Windows and menus on Apple silicon Mac. Linux support is experimental. The alpha tags contain no downloadable player.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Prepare your own base and matching update or a merged 13.0.5 dump, prod.keys, title.keys and firmware NCAs for fonts. XCI, NSP and NCA are accepted. Expand NSZ or XCZ first.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Use the project’s source installer to download the tools and compile locally. The primary Windows 10 or 11 x64 route needs AVX2 and Direct3D 11. Mac source targets Apple silicon on macOS 14.5 or later.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
The Linux route uses DXVK and Vulkan, with CPU support unconfirmed. Two-PC matches, physical controllers and the online roster have limited testing.

Limitations and evidence

  • Keep the original game data after compiling. The player may still need it during play.
