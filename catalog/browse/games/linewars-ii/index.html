Upstream availability claim
packages

Only the reviewed freeware v1.0.0 Windows x64 ZIP and Linux x64 archive can be downloaded, in a Portcove version that checks their exact files. Other files and releases are not yet reviewed. You can also get the package from the publisher, keep its notices and register your extracted copy.

A release may contain research reports rather than a playable package.

Setup

  1. Extract the publisher package

    Choose the Windows x64 ZIP or the Linux x64 archive. The Linux build requires glibc 2.39 or later. Keep game.dat and the matching SDL3 library in the LineWars2 folder with the player.

  2. Register the extracted player

    In Portcove, select linewars2.exe on Windows or linewars2 on Linux. Use the writable LineWars2 folder as the working directory. Review the registration, then select Play.

Setup notes
Keep your settings
The player stores settings.dat beside its executable. When replacing a copy, preserve that file, the complete working folder and the separate LW2/LineWars2 preferences folder.

Limitations and evidence

  • The original game and its data are supplied with permission for freeware use. Keep the bundled notices. The player includes the AT&T PC 6300 and IBM VGA bitmap fonts.
