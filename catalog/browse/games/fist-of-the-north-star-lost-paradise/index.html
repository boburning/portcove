Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own Fist of the North Star: Lost Paradise PS4 CUSA12781 v01.00 dump with eboot.bin/sce_sys. CUSA10053 Asian edition is only an experimental prologue→first save report, not equivalent source qualification.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Extract the release ZIP into a writable folder, or build with MSYS2 UCRT64 and build-win.sh. In the launcher, select your own CUSA12781 folder to prepare the boot image without modifying the originals. The alternate Asian edition has not been confirmed. The recorded review did not inspect an archive.

Limitations and evidence

  • The project reports early-alpha exploration, combat and save/load near 60 FPS on one Windows 11/RTX5070 machine, with remaining drops and defects. A complete campaign and alternate original-file editions have not been confirmed.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
