Setup

  1. Choose data from your original game

    Use your original DOS data file SPAE.PA, SPAD.PA, SPAF.PA or SPAU.PA, or the six original Amiga files listed in the upstream guide. Copy them to ~/.local/share/freeserf on Linux or Mac, or to the freeserf folder under %LOCALAPPDATA% on Windows, and keep your originals separate. When launching from a terminal, -g takes the directory containing your data.

  2. Install the Windows or Mac release

    On Windows x64, use the Release installer. On Apple Silicon with macOS 26 or later, copy FreeSerf.app from the DMG. Set up your original data before opening the player.

  3. Keep your saves and settings

    FreeSerf keeps saves, including Ctrl+Z quicksaves and the optional autosave, in freeserf/saves under XDG_DATA_HOME on Linux, or in ~/freeserf/saves when XDG_DATA_HOME is unset. On Windows it uses the freeserf folder in your Saved Games folder, and on Mac Library/Application Support/freeserf/saves. Settings are in freeserf.ini: in ~/.local/share/freeserf or under XDG_DATA_HOME on Linux, in %APPDATA%\freeserf on Windows, and in Library/Application Support/freeserf on Mac. These stay in place when the player is replaced. Use -l with a saved game file to reopen it.

Limitations and evidence

  • The v0.5 Mac DMG requires Apple Silicon. The publisher does not provide an Intel Mac or Android package in this release.
