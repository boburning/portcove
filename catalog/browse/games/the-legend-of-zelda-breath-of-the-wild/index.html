Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Use title 01007EF00011E000, the matching 1.6.0 or 1.9.0 update, your prod.keys and firmware or shared fonts. The project accepts combined 1.6.0 cards with DLC or a separate base and update without DLC. Supply any 1.9.0 DLC separately as titles 01007EF00011F001 and 01007EF00011F002.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Use the project’s Python 3.14 build_app.py to build the Mac app or an IPA to sign and install. The Mac needs Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later and Metal 4. The iPhone route needs iOS 26 or later and an iPhone 14 or later.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
The project reports Mac opening scenes, saves and selected DLC scenes. Sound and performance vary by scene and version. Its iOS 1.9.0 build has no play report.

Limitations and evidence

  • Back up the profile before rebuilding or launching an update. Keep the backup if you might return to the older version.

    Prepare the 1.9.0 ExeFS with main, main.npdm, rtld, sdk and subsdk0, plus the complete merged RomFS. The launcher does not decrypt a game file or supply keys, firmware or extraction tools.
