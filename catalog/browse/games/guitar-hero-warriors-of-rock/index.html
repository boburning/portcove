Connect the supported XINPUT-compatible guitar or gamepad before launching, as required by this upstream player; no physical-controller qualification is claimed here.

    Select Guitar Hero Warriors of Rock.exe, the complete prepared package folder as working directory and no extra arguments for the normal direct native player. Keep only supported literal arguments already needed by your working setup. Do not select re-wor-launcher.exe or a script.

  3. Review launch details, then choose Play

    Review the selected game executable, working folder and launch arguments, then agree to use them. Choose Play separately when you are ready.
