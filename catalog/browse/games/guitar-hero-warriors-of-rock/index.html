Setup

  1. Prepare your own supported original upstream

    Use your own legitimate Xbox 360 Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock original, retaining assets/default.xex and assets/data, including streams and scripts. Do not substitute another Guitar Hero game. Complete supported extraction upstream; this guide imports no original and runs no helper. Connect the supported XINPUT-compatible guitar or gamepad before launching, as required by this upstream player; no physical-controller qualification is claimed here.

  2. Attach the completed native Windows player

    Select Guitar Hero Warriors of Rock.exe, the complete prepared package folder as working directory and no extra arguments for the normal direct native player. Keep only supported literal arguments already needed by your working setup. Do not select re-wor-launcher.exe or a script.

  3. Review launch details, then choose Play

    Review the selected game executable, working folder and launch arguments, then agree to use them. Choose Play separately when you are ready. Adding this copy does not prepare, update or replace its external files. Removing its registration leaves all external files in place. Before changing versions, close the game and its companion programs and keep the working copy and all data. Review and select the changed executable before playing again.
