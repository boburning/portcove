Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own Xbox 360 F.E.A.R. files, title ID 565507D9, with default.xex and Game/ under port-nx/assets/. The exact region, revision and hash are not confirmed in the README; check docs/SETUP.md before preparing files. F.E.A.R. Files, title ID 565507E8, is a separate game. Optional PC textures require your own corresponding PC files.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Upstream v0.1.0 releases are source only. On Windows, prepare the patched SDK with tools/setup-sdk.ps1 and locally build the base game with tools/build-nx.ps1 from the matching original disc. tools/launch.ps1 -Collection is an optional shared game-selection route after each title is separately prepared. No public compiled game/player archive is offered by the README; original files stay in the local assets folder.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream reports startup, menus, intro videos, campaign and Instant Action, high-frame-rate fixes and checkpoint/Continue support. A full base campaign and the Xbox 360 bonus mission remain unconfirmed. GPU spikes in Interval 2, soft fonts and effects/performance issues remain. Steam Deck testing uses experimental Proton; native Linux is untested and Android ARM64 work has not started. Portcove has not confirmed runtime behavior or gameplay.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
