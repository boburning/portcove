Setup

  1. Use the exact original files

    Use your own MediEvil II (USA) disc (SCUS-94564) with both of its tracks and your own retail SCPH-1001 BIOS. This setup kit doesn’t include OpenBIOS. Portcove checks the files and builds the player.

Limitations and evidence

  • This route doesn’t include the widescreen, frame-rate and control mods of the prebuilt MediEvil II player. Choose the prebuilt route if you want them.
  • Portcove checks the first data track and track count. Keep the complete disc intact.
