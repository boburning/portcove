Setup

  1. Use the exact original files

    Use your own Alone in the Dark: The New Nightmare (Europe) two-disc set (SLES-02801, SLES-12801), each disc with its data track and its audio track, and your own retail SCPH-5552 BIOS. Portcove checks the files and builds the player.

Limitations and evidence

  • Portcove checks each disc's data track and track count. Keep every disc intact, with both of its tracks.
  • Native build, disc switching, music playback, gameplay and save compatibility remain unobserved.
