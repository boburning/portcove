Setup

  1. Prepare upstream or keep an existing game build

    Use the Linux developer quick start with your own Xbox NTSC-U TimeSplitters: Future Perfect disc. The supported extracted default.xbe SHA-256 is 3cfd001a84fc3e08175d6c4b2e42ebf49577a089b5d0bd87ac724f61a41816bc; the reviewed guide does not support other dumps. Its XDVDFS/code-generation steps and local build are deliberate upstream actions. Use the documented native play invocation with --disc and its interactive/Vulkan options, preserving the prepared working folder and data. Register only that prepared native runtime. Other platforms and full-game behavior need separate evidence.

  2. Keep your files and update through upstream

    Keep the game in a separate folder you control, and retain a working copy. Review its executable in Portcove, then add it without changing its files. Choose Play separately when you are ready. You manage updates, original files, generated data, saves and settings. Portcove does not replace, back up or roll back this external copy. Follow the project’s instructions for the version you use when changing it.
