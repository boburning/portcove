Pokémon: Let’s Go, Pikachu! (letsgo-recomp)

Build Let’s Go, Pikachu! locally with the project’s Windows launcher and files from your own game. Version 1.0.0 enables all options, although Pikachu has less testing than Eevee. Online play, a second player and Poké Ball Plus are unavailable.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Extract the Pikachu edition’s ExeFS and RomFS from your own XCI or NSP before starting. The guide accepts any region. Versions 1.0.1 and 1.0.2 have fewer options and less testing.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Use a separate folder for Pikachu in the Windows 10 or 11 x64 launcher. It imports and recompiles locally once. Your PC needs SSE4.2 and Vulkan 1.3, with AVX2 recommended.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
A full playthrough and other native desktop systems are unconfirmed. Higher frame rates, frame generation and wild battles are experimental. The extracted-file route lists no separate keys or firmware.
