Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA or Europe Xbox 360 game, title ID 555307D5. This guide has only an abbreviated default.xex SHA-256, 295ac4de...c023a; check the full upstream requirement before setup. Keep the main, multiplayer and campaign executables together as one game installation.

  2. Choose the upstream setup route

    On Steam Deck or a Linux x86-64 system with AVX2, FMA and Vulkan 1.3, extract the v0.6 Linux ZIP and follow its README.txt to supply your owned disc files. The publisher reports testing on Steam Deck. On Windows, follow the v0.6 source-build instructions. Keep your game files and saves before updating.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream reports campaign missions playable in v0.6. Earlier builds reported black 3D output and unfinished campaign startup. Check the v0.6 release notes for current limitations.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
