Setup

  1. Select your original in the game

    Choose Play, then select your own Donkey Kong Country USA v1.0 dump in the game’s picker. The published Windows player includes its optional Dixie companion and requires no compiler or separate installer. Follow the game’s own options for choosing that companion or an optional music pack. Follow the included upstream license terms, including its framework’s noncommercial-use limit.

  2. Set up the Apple Silicon preview

    Use the upstream Apple Silicon HD Preview ZIP on macOS 26 or later. Extract the complete app outside your Portcove library and select your own clean Donkey Kong Country USA v1.0 ROM. The HD option covers Jungle Hijinxs and its connected rooms. Keep your existing Mac saves before replacing the app.
