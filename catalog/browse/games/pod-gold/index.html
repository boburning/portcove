A release may contain research reports rather than a playable package.

Setup

  1. Prepare your owned POD Gold copy

    On Windows 10 or 11 x64, supply your POD Gold CD, a complete working copy of its files containing PODMMX.EXE and DATA, or your POD2_0.BIN image with its CUE. The upstream setup can also read an ISO of the supported disc. Retain the originals.

  2. Build outside Portcove

    Use the upstream installer or tagged Setup.cmd and complete its build first. The installer is a setup program and can install Python, CMake, Ninja, and Visual Studio 2022 Build Tools. Review prerequisite installation in the upstream setup. This produces build/pod.exe in the preparation folder.

  3. Register the completed player

    Select build/pod.exe with the preparation folder containing build and game as its working directory. Add --run as a separate argument and choose Play. POD Gold (recomp).cmd is the upstream shortcut and starts another process.

Limitations and evidence

  • Keep a separate backup of the whole game working folder, including game/UbiSoft/Ubi.ini, before rebuilding. The active working copy and generated player belong to your upstream setup.
  • The setup application is x64. Its generated game player is Win32 and runs on compatible Windows x64. This release has no native Linux, Mac, or Android setup path.
