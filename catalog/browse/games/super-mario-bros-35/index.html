Super Mario Bros. 35 (sm35recomp)

Use an already-owned Super Mario Bros. 35 game and its 1.0.2 update with the project’s launcher. The project reports two-player matching between Mac and iPad through Nextendo’s community servers. Only one device per internet connection can join a match.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Provide your own base game and 1.0.2 update as NSP or XCI, or extracted ExeFS and RomFS. The launcher checks the version and needs your prod.keys and firmware.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Choose a launcher for Apple silicon macOS 15 or later, Windows x64 with AVX2, or Linux x64 with AVX2 or aarch64. Setup builds locally. Online matches use a Nextendo community account.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
The project reports iOS source play. It has not tested Android source, Windows or Linux hardware, long sessions or large matches. Availability of a new lawful copy of the original game is unconfirmed.
