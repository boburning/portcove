Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA SLUS-00797 R4 CHD and SCPH1001 BIOS. Keep your original game files separately. The recorded source review did not download or run the game.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Follow the project’s extract_chd and build-macos instructions, then start R4_Recompiled. Capture a race and use compile-race-overlay-macos.sh to prepare its native cache as documented. Portcove does not manage this download and has not confirmed setup or gameplay.

Limitations and evidence

  • The project documents game-specific frame-split addresses and timing, targeting 60 FPS, widescreen and analog controls. Broader replay, audio and FMV support remain unverified. Portcove has not confirmed setup or gameplay.
