Setup

  1. Prepare your original game

    Before first Play, copy your own complete X-COM: Apocalypse disc image into the package’s data folder as cd.iso. Upstream also supports an extracted disc folder named cd.iso. For the GOG CUE/BIN pair, rename a copy of XCOM.cue to cd.iso and keep the referenced XCOM.BIN alongside it. Retain your originals. This package is the portable engine; no installer or local game build is needed. Upstream requires OpenGL 2.0 and the Visual C++ runtime.
