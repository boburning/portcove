Setup

  1. Supply both Japanese discs and the BIOS

    Choose the folder with your two original Japanese CHDs and your retail SCPH-5500 BIOS. Name the CHDs so Disc 1 sorts before Disc 2. Portcove checks both discs and builds the game on this computer.

  2. Choose the disc before Play

    If the game asks for the other disc, close it and choose that disc beside Play before starting it again.
