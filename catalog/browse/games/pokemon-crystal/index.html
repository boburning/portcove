Setup

  1. Open the official player

    On Windows, extract the official Windows ZIP and open the player with its supplied companions. On Linux, open the executable AppImage for your CPU or follow the upstream Flatpak instructions. On Intel or Apple Silicon Mac, extract the official Mac ZIP and open the complete gen1recomp.app. On Android, use the official APK.

  2. Import your original

    In Gen1Recomp, import your own canonical US English Pokémon Crystal ROM. Follow the upstream README for accepted revisions. The importer checks the original and may start the game immediately. Use this title’s tab for later Play.

  3. Keep your game data

    Use the upstream player for updates. Back up your actual saves, imports, settings and profile separately before changing versions.

Limitations and evidence

  • Keep the original outside the player’s working data. Gen1Recomp owns the imported data and profile.
