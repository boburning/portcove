Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA RENE8P, Europe RENP8P or Japan RENJ8P disc in ISO, RVZ, WIA or WBFS form. The first extraction needs about 3.5 GB.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    The release executable includes the translated game code; follow its installer instructions with your own disc data. The installer retains the extracted data. Building from source is a separate route that reads your own discs.
