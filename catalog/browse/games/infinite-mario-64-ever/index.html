Setup

  1. Select your original in the game

    Choose Play, then select your own Super Mario 64 USA .z64 dump in the game’s ROM picker. The player verifies the original before loading it; the published Windows and Linux archives already contain the Godot player and its required companions. The Linux player and its bundled libraries need glibc 2.38 or later and GCC 13’s libstdc++.
