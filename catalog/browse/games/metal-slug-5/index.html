Metal Slug 5 (Neo Geo) / Metal-Slug-5-Recomp (omegakatana92)

Native static recompilation of Metal Slug 5 for Windows x64 and Steam Deck/Linux x86_64. The Neo Geo 68000 program runs as recompiled C against a modeled MVS board, with Z80/YM2610 audio, SDL2 input, and a Steam Deck AppImage. Verified boot, coin/start and Mission 1; continuous audio. Source only; no ROMs or BIOS. PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.
