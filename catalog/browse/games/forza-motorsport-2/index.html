Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own European Xbox 360 Forza Motorsport 2 ISO and matching mandatory TU_16L61VA_... title update. DLC is optional and must be your own. This guide has only an abbreviated default.xex SHA-256, 9726066f...9b120a; obtain the full XEX and title-update hashes from the importer before setup.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Use FM2-Setup-0.3.0.exe to import your ISO, title update and optional DLC. It builds local game files and installs portable Python. The launcher requires .NET 10 Desktop Runtime, installed with Microsoft’s installer if missing, and WebView2. Use Windows 10/11 x64 with Direct3D 12 feature level 12_0. This is the native kernel/XAM and ReGPU project, a separate implementation from ReXGlue-based Forza 2.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream reports startup, intro, menus, races and save profiles, with hardware-specific performance. High-frame-rate, 4K and ultrawide claims have not been confirmed by Portcove. Racing-wheel support is beta.
