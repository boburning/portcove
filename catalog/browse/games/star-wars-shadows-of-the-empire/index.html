Setup

  1. Keep your permitted Windows copy together

    For an already permitted 0.9.7 or 0.9.8 Windows copy, keep its program and companion files together. Put your own USA Rev 2 original beside Shadows of the Empire.exe as sote.us.v1.2.z64. Verify upstream launch in that folder, then select the executable and its containing folder in Portcove. Save the details before choosing Play.

  2. Check the experimental Linux requirements

    Keep the permitted Linux x64 kit on a Linux filesystem. Put your matching USA Rev 2 ROM in that folder as sote.us.v1.2.z64, then run ./Play.sh from the folder. Keep its bundled libraries beside the launcher. The game needs glibc 2.43 or newer, libstdc++ with GLIBCXX_3.4.32, and Vulkan. The publisher’s 0.9.7 Linux run reached early gameplay and later showed solid-blue output. That issue remains open for the experimental Linux route.

Setup notes
Preserve the working copy and player data
Before changing kits, back up your saves, settings and custom texture packs. Also keep your optional PC movies, voices and music in Sdata. When updating an already permitted copy to 0.9.8, use the matching program files and two bundled pack folders from the same release. Keep other custom packs separately. Keep the old working copy until you have checked your progress in the new one.

Limitations and evidence

  • The release’s MIT grant applies to eligible project code. Use a copy and bundled components you are already permitted to use. Fresh package acquisition remains held.
