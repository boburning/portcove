Setup notes
Prepare your disc once and keep the memory cards
Open timesplitters.exe on Windows or ./timesplitters on Linux from the extracted folder. Put your own USA SLUS-200.90 PS2 dump in that folder. Choose Launch game to extract it on the first run. Later launches reuse game-data. Keep all of game-data, including mc0 and mc1, and keep timesplitters.ini when you replace the upstream package. The Linux build needs glibc 2.43 or later, OpenGL 3.3 and X11.
Start directly using saved Linux settings
After completing upstream setup, use ./timesplitters --no-launcher to resume with the saved settings. Open ./timesplitters without the switch to return to the settings launcher.
