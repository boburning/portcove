Setup

  1. Open the Linux AppImage

    Choose the official x86-64, ARM64 or Steam Deck package for your device. Mark the AppImage executable and open it. Check the upstream platform guide for your release’s Vulkan and system-library requirements.

  2. Add your original game

    In Play, choose Add a ROM and select your own clean US v1.0 or US Rev A/v1.1 original. Some older mods need US v1.0 when you install them.

Setup notes
Find your DKR-R profile
Linux normally uses $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/dkr-port or ~/.config/dkr-port. Portable mode uses dkr-runtime-data beside the application. Changing modes selects a different profile on the next launch. Use DKR-R’s Save Manager to export progress and back up your settings and installed content separately.

Limitations and evidence

  • Use DKR-R’s upstream launcher and update instructions for your Linux copy. Back up the active profile before replacing the package or switching profile locations.
