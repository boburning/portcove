Setup

  1. Start the game

    The official archive includes the Gladiator campaigns and the native game. Choose Play after installation; no original commercial files or separate setup program are required. openscen.exe is the optional level editor, not this route’s play target.

  2. Prepare Linux x64

    Install the upstream player's system libraries before choosing Play. On Ubuntu 26.04 the tested package needs libphysfs1 and libzip5 in addition to its other system libraries, including OpenSSL 3. Keep your ~/.openglad profile separately. It contains your saves, settings and campaigns and is outside Portcove backups.

  3. Prepare your Apple silicon Mac

    The inspected ARM64 player needs macOS 26 and the Apple silicon Homebrew libraries libyaml, physfs, libzip and sdl3 under /opt/homebrew. Follow the upstream Mac setup for those libraries before Play. The managed player opens from its own folder and keeps your saves, campaigns and settings under ~/.openglad.

Limitations and evidence

  • Use a separately prepared copy for OPENGLAD_CONFIG_DIR or a custom Mac library search path. Keep the actual OpenGlad profile and its creating runtime before changing versions.
