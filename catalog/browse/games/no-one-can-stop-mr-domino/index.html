Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA SLUS-00804 MrDomino disc in CUE form, with OpenBIOS or your own SCPH1001 BIOS. Keep your original files separately.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Follow scripts/build_windows.ps1 to generate and compile the game. Captured overlays compile with MinGW GCC. Prepare and build the game yourself; Portcove does not install it automatically and has not confirmed setup or gameplay.

Limitations and evidence
