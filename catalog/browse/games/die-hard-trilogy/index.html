Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA SLUS00119 or PAL SLES00445 Die Hard Trilogy disc. The importer checks its exact CRC and boot SHA256, but does not verify every overlay or audio file. Keep your original game files separately. The recorded source review did not download or run the game.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Follow the advertised v0.2.0 package’s Play.cmd and DISCS setup with your own disc. Keep the separate dht and dht-pal runtimes, settings and cards with their matching editions. A local source build is also available. Portcove does not manage this download and has not confirmed setup or gameplay.
