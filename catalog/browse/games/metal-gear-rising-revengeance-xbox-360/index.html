Setup notes
Use the Zandatsu Android APK
Install Zandatsu-v1.3.apk on ARM64 with Android 11 or later and Vulkan 1.3. Open the app and accept its suggested graphics profile or choose one yourself. In Home, open Game files and import your own USA or Europe Xbox 360 ISO. Keep it where you selected it. In Drivers, install and enable the driver if the app asks for one. Tap Play. Use the app to export or restore saves. The publisher says installing its newer APK over the older one preserves game files, saves and settings.
