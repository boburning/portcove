Setup

  1. Prepare your disc and matching setup kit

    Use an unmodified Tobal 2 Japan v1.1 disc (SLPM-86033). Keep its single-track BIN and paired CUE together. The BIN SHA-256 is 04e79535b4616963e045957b4f9b87126753e94b01d35d32c60214df04bf5a5a. Extract the v0.2.6 kit for Windows x64, Linux x64, Intel Mac or Apple Silicon into a writable folder.

  2. Build the game on your computer

    Open Tobal_2_Recompiled from the extracted kit (Tobal_2_Recompiled.exe on Windows). On Mac install Apple’s Command Line Tools first. Select your CUE, choose Generate & rebuild and allow Internet access for first-run tools. After the build, choose English or Japanese and press PLAY in the upstream launcher.

  3. Keep your memory cards before changing kits

    Preserve the extracted kit and its saves and build-release/saves folders wherever they exist. Keep the original disc files separately. Upstream reports that memory cards have yet to be verified in every mode.
