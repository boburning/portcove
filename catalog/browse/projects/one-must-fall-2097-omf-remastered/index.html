Setup

  1. Choose the upstream game package

    Choose an OMF-2097-Remastered file for your system from the upstream release. The OMF-Studio files open the separate editor. The game includes its freeware resources and starts without importing a DOS installation.

  2. Start the Windows or Linux player

    On Windows 10 or 11, run the matching upstream game EXE with WebView2 available. The release also offers Windows ARM64 and 32-bit Windows 10 builds. On Linux, choose the x86-64 or AArch64 AppImage, make it executable and launch it. Use your distribution’s FUSE 2 package if the AppImage requires it.

  3. Open the Mac app

    Choose the separate Intel or Apple Silicon ZIP for your Mac. Extract it and keep the complete OMF 2097 Remastered.app bundle. Both packages require macOS 12 or later. The upstream release is unsigned on Windows and lacks Apple notarization on Mac.

  4. Play in your browser

    Use the Play Online link on the upstream project on a computer, tablet or phone. Keep the site data in the browser profile you play in. Android uses this browser route.

Setup notes
Preserve your player profile
Keep the WebView profile with your controls, settings and replays before replacing an upstream desktop copy. On Linux it is under $XDG_DATA_HOME/com.omf2097.remastered, normally ~/.local/share/com.omf2097.remastered. Browser play uses that browser’s separate site data.

Limitations and evidence

  • The upstream packages bundle the freeware original and remastered art used during play. Portcove Download is held for the bundled gameplay art. Use the publisher’s own game page and keep your desktop or browser copy under your control.
