Setup

  1. Prepare upstream or keep an existing game build

    Use your own God of War NTSC-U SCUS-97399 files, including both DVD layers and the required IRX/.disc_layout files beside the ELF. Follow upstream's documented 1_instalar_herramientas and 2_compilar preparation scripts deliberately; 3_ejecutar_manual.cmd is its untimed manual launch. Register ps2EntryRunner.exe only when its explicit working folder, data and invocation reproduce the prepared native runtime. Portcove does not import a setup script's environment or run its compiler during discovery. The current upstream save/load and full-completion work remains in progress.

  2. Keep your files and update through upstream

    Keep the game in a separate folder you control, and retain a working copy. Review its executable in Portcove, then add it without changing its files. Choose Play separately when you are ready. You manage updates, original files, generated data, saves and settings. Portcove does not replace, back up or roll back this external copy. Follow the project’s instructions for the version you use when changing it.
