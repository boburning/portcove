Setup

  1. Select your ICO disc on first Play

    Choose Play, then select your own ICO European SCES-50760, Japanese SCPS-11003 or Korean SCPS-56001 disc image when asked. Use ISO, CHD, or BIN with its matching CUE; the US disc is not supported. Keep the image outside the runtime folder. The game verifies and extracts its data on first launch (about 1 GB); later launches use that extracted copy. Keep your original image available if a future data-format change requires extraction again.

  2. Keep saves with this portable copy

    The official portable download enables portable=1 and keeps saves, settings and extracted disc data in userdata beside the player. Windows puts the player in x64; Linux puts it at the archive root. Back up userdata/memcard for saves, and use userdata/memcard2 for an optional second card. With portable=0 or an unwritable portable folder, data instead uses the upstream user-profile folder. Preserve that folder separately. Texture/model packs go in the documented textures/models folders. Close the game and preserve your current data before replacing a runtime; older versions may not read newer saves.

  3. Other upstream platforms

    The upstream project also publishes Apple-silicon macOS bundles, ARM Linux and mobile packages. Follow its platform guide for those copies; their setup and data locations differ from these Windows x64 and Linux x64 portable archives.
