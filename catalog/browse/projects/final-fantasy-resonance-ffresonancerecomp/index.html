FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE (FFResonanceRecomp)

An experimental FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE port running statically rewritten ARM64 code on Apple Silicon with nxnative-based NVN-to-Metal support. The project reports early title, story, town and battle play at 60 FPS. Eight of 73496 shaders fail; compute effects are missing, night scenes are dark and crashes remain possible. There are no releases. The make build source route needs Xcode MetalToolchain, Homebrew, .NET, Python and 60 GB of space, with an estimated two-hour first shader build. Only Apple Silicon M1+ with macOS 26+ is supported; Intel Mac, Windows and Linux are explicitly unsupported. Use your own base NSP 0100606026F12000 and 1.0.1 update NSP 0100606026F12800 v65536, plus prod.keys. Rewritten code, shaders and icons stay local. Firmware needs, region and hashes are unconfirmed. Portcove offers no setup or launch integration and has not tested gameplay.
