Pokémon: Let’s Go, Pikachu! (letsgo-recomp)

An in-development Pokémon: Let’s Go port translating AArch64 code to C with an LAS-based Vulkan player. Eevee 1.0.0 has more testing than Pikachu; a full playthrough is unconfirmed. Online, Poké Ball Plus and player 2 are unsupported. Higher frame rates, frame generation and wild battles are experimental. The Windows ZIP launcher imports, copies and recompiles your game locally once; keep one folder per game. Windows 10/11 x64 needs SSE4.2 and Vulkan 1.3, with AVX2 recommended. Other native desktop routes are unconfirmed. Supply your own corresponding Eevee or Pikachu extracted ExeFS/RomFS from any region. Base 1.0.0 enables all options; 1.0.1/1.0.2 have fewer options and less testing. Extract XCI/NSP externally first. No separate keys/firmware input is described after extraction. Portcove offers no setup or launch integration and has not tested gameplay.
