Setup

  1. Prepare upstream or keep an existing game build

    Review upstream's exact component permission conditions before a local build or use: reconstructed src/include status is unsettled, and the port's CC0 and third-party component notices do not grant rights to the originals. If you have an established basis for that operation, follow the source guide with your own G4QE01 USA Super Mario Strikers disc. Its player is build-pc/bin/AuroraStrikers.exe with --disc and your absolute image path; keep the prepared project working directory and arguments. Upstream prohibits redistribution of the game-derived built executable. Portcove does not build or distribute it, and these instructions grant no permission.

  2. Keep your files and update through upstream

    Keep the game in a separate folder you control, and retain a working copy. Review its executable in Portcove, then add it without changing its files. Choose Play separately when you are ready. You manage updates, original files, generated data, saves and settings. Portcove does not replace, back up or roll back this external copy. Follow the project’s instructions for the version you use when changing it.
