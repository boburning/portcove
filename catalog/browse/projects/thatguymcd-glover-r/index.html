Setup

  1. Select your own Glover ROM on first Play

    Choose Play, then select your own unmodified Glover USA revision 0 ROM when the game asks. The launcher accepts .z64, .n64 and .v64 byte order for this 8 MiB NGVE cartridge (canonical SHA-1 270be17b3c8da9b88a7b99c2a545b0bce16837f4). The published player needs no game build or separate installer. Configure controls in the launcher or press F1/Escape while playing; use its Mods page for compatible mods and separate mod profiles.

  2. Keep your saves and mod profiles safe

    Default Windows saves, settings, mod profiles, mod-specific saves, logs and crash reports use %APPDATA%/Glover-R. Keep a separate backup of that profile before changing versions; an older runtime may not read newer data. Optional custom configuration and developer paths need their own backup. Keep customized existing copies registered with their own launch details.

  3. Choose the Linux package for your system

    Portcove manages the direct x86-64 AppImage with working AppImage and FUSE support. To maintain a separate Linux copy, follow the upstream AppImage or Portable archive instructions. Steam Deck has its own package and START-GLOVER-R.sh. On Linux, keep your settings, saves and mods from $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/glover-r or ~/.config/glover-r separately backed up.
