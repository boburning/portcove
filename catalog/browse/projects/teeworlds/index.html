Setup

  1. Install the client

    On Windows, extract the publisher ZIP and open teeworlds.exe. On Linux, install Teeworlds through your package manager or unpack the publisher archive and launch teeworlds from its folder. The community Flathub package is available on Linux x64 and ARM64. The publisher’s Mac disk image contains an Intel client.

  2. Play and record a match

    The Teeworlds 0.7 client connects to Teeworlds 0.7 servers. In a match, press F1 and enter record followed by a demo name. Enter stoprecord to finish, then open the recording from Demos.

Limitations and evidence

  • Before moving your setup, close the game and copy the data folder with settings07.cfg, demos, custom maps, and any skins you want to keep.
