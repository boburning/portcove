Setup

  1. Finish setup in the upstream Android app

    Install this fork’s Android APK, open it, and choose your own Mario Kart 64 US .z64 ROM. The upstream guide identifies SHA-1 579c48e211ae952530ffc8738709f078d5dd215e. This app verifies the ROM and creates mk64.o2r on the device; no PC Spaghettify/Torch step is required.

  2. Keep your data when changing versions

    The Android guide puts game data under Android/data/com.izzy.kart/files. Mods go in its mods folder as supported .o2r, .zip or plain folders and load next launch; .otr is not supported. Preserve your app data, saves, generated O2R and mods before updating or removing the app. Android/upstream owns replacement and storage; Portcove backup and runtime rollback do not cover it.
