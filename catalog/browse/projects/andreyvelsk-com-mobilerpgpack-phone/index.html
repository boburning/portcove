Setup

  1. Finish setup in the upstream Android app

    This fork’s published APK is for Android, specifically the AYN Thor dual-screen UZDoom route. Install this fork’s APK on the Android device, open its launcher deliberately, and select your own Doom or Doom II IWAD through the UZDoom setup. Other engines named in the inherited parent README have been excluded from this fork; do not prepare Doom RPG, Wolfenstein RPG or another parent engine for this APK.

  2. Keep your data when changing versions

    Keep your selected IWADs, mods and actual Android app data backed up before changing versions. This fork’s reviewed guide does not establish an exact save/export location. Its UZDoom renderer notes distinguish Adreno from Mali/PowerVR; follow the fork’s device guidance. Updates and device setup belong to the upstream Android app, outside Portcove runtime backup and rollback.
