WipEout Omega Collection — wipeout_pc (AbdelmoumenDeghbouche)

An experimental port of WipEout Omega Collection for owned PS4 game files, with a documented US target of CUSA07671 and update 01.07. The publisher offers a Windows ZIP and Linux x86-64 AppImage and reports HD and 2048 races. A full collection playthrough remains unconfirmed.

Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own PS4 WipEout Omega Collection dump. The README names USA CUSA07671 base 01.00 with update 01.07 as its development target; it also names European CUSA05670 without specifying a version. Merge your own base and update files or supply GOW3_PATCH_DIR as documented. GoldHEN folder dumps are also supported by the instructions. The runtime reads your eboot.bin and game files and links your libc, Fios2 and NpToolkit2. One executable serves 2048, HD and Fury. Exact supported executable and asset fingerprints are not established.

  2. Choose the upstream player

    On Windows use the publisher’s wipeout-windows.zip. On Linux x86-64 use the publisher’s AppImage and check its system requirements. Open the upstream player and select your owned game folder containing eboot.bin. Follow the upstream instructions for your disc edition and update data. Only the publisher’s package listing was reviewed.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
The project reports 110–150 FPS in 2048 on an RTX 4070 Laptop GPU. From v0.2.3, the experimental GOW3_NEO=1 setting displayed a race, with no further testing reported. Unchecked areas include HD Fury and other events, HDR and some audio effects. PSVR is outside the project’s plans.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream early-development status claims boot/menus/saves and HD/2048 races/results, with 110–150FPS reported in 2048 on RTX 4070 Laptop, not a full collection playthrough. New README reports extra render-resolution patches and experimental PS4 Pro mode GOW3_NEO=1 loading a race with correct picture since v0.2.3; not tested further. All HD/HD Fury/2048 event types are not yet tested, HDR RCAS fix awaits testing and new audio effects may have defects; PSVR is not planned. The upstream Linux x86-64 AppImage is listed in the release; Linux gameplay and CPU compatibility remain unverified. No Portcove runtime or completion qualification was performed.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
