Setup

  1. Load your original game on first Play

    Choose Play, then Load ROM in the player and select your own USA Beetle Adventure Racing cartridge dump. The player checks its expected game identity and keeps its own copy of the selected game in its profile, so keep your original separately. The released player needs no decompilation build or separate game-generation step.

  2. Keep a complete Windows player

    For a copy you manage yourself, extract the whole Windows x64 alpha ZIP and keep beetle-adventure-racing-recomp.exe with its assets and DLLs; debug-symbols ZIPs are not player packages. To add that copy to Portcove, select the player and its folder.

Limitations and evidence

  • The package includes a modified RecompFrontend copy that the project license does not cover. Portcove downloads it only from the project’s own releases. You need your own original game files.
  • This alpha has upstream limitations, and Portcove has not observed native setup, gameplay or saves. Linux is not offered.
