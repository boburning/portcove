Setup notes
Developer renderer preparation details
Follow upstream’s developer instructions with your own disc/main.dol only if you are preparing or retaining this experimental renderer; preserve its working directory and documented --iso arguments.
Maintenance and registration scope
You or upstream keep its updates, original files, generated data, saves and settings. Portcove does not supply a playable package, replace this runtime, or back it up or roll it back.

Limitations and evidence

  • This project currently demonstrates the Wind Waker renderer and framework, with the frame loop driven manually; it is not yet the game. Its Windows ww_launcher.exe is that development renderer.
