Setup

  1. Choose your existing permitted player

    Keep the program and its graphics, sound and level folders together. On Windows, use the x64 or x86 package matching your system and start Blips.exe from its own folder. On Linux, use the AppImage or Ubuntu ZIP for x86-64 or ARM64. Make the AppImage executable. If FUSE is unavailable, add --appimage-extract-and-run to the AppImage command.

  2. Choose the correct Mac build

    On Intel Mac with macOS 15 or later, use Blips-macos-15-intel_intel.zip. On Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later, use Blips-macos-14_arm64.zip. Open Blips.app from the extracted folder.

Setup notes
Preserve progress, settings and custom levels
Keep .blips_settings, .blips_joystick.def, .blips_*.dat and .blips_levelpacks from the player’s HOME directory. If HOME is unset, look in the directory you started the player from. Keep the old working copy until you confirm your data in a replacement.

Limitations and evidence

  • Use an existing copy you already trust and are permitted to use. Keep its asset notices with the player.
