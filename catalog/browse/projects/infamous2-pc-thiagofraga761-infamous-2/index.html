Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own inFAMOUS 2 PlayStation 3 BCUS98125 version 01.00 disc tree with decrypted PS3_GAME/USRDIR/EBOOT.elf and your own PS3 dev_flash firmware. Keep generated game code local.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    Follow scripts/setup.sh to fetch the pinned ps3recomp and title patch. Use lift.sh to translate your own PPU/SPU EBOOT, then build.sh to create work/port/build-vk. Launch with your disc through the documented work/play.sh route.
