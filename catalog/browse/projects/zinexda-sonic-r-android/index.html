Setup

  1. Finish setup in the upstream Android app

    Install this fork’s Android APK and use its guided asset installer to choose your own complete retail Sonic R PC game folder. This is the Android GLES2/SDL2 wrapper; a Windows player or another Sonic game’s files do not replace the required retail data. Choose Play through the upstream app after it accepts the folder.

  2. Keep your data when changing versions

    Keep your original game files and preserve the Android app’s actual save/config data before changing or removing it. The selected guide does not establish the exact save/export location. Multiplayer is a planned feature, not a delivered cross-platform promise. The upstream custom license permits free download/build/modification and forbids sale; this route retains those terms and upstream update ownership. Portcove does not manage APK replacement or its data backup.
