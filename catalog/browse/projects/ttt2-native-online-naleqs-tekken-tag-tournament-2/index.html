Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own USA or European Xbox 360 Tekken Tag Tournament 2 ISO or extracted folder. Setup checks all game-file checksums and rejects modified or unsupported revisions. TU3 and DLC are optional and must come from your own files; older title updates are ignored. This guide does not include the exact supported fingerprint manifest.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    In the release TTT2NativeOnline.exe, choose Setup the game, or follow the source Setup.bat procedure. It checks and copies your files, downloads an approximately 250 MB portable compiler checked against its expected SHA-256, translates Default.xex and compiles into native/bin. The player targets Windows 10/11 x64 with AVX2 and D3D11. Rebuilding after code updates retains verified data.

Limitations and evidence

  • This early build reaches native menus at 60 FPS, selection, intros, fights and local versus. Online play uses lockstep and input delay; rollback is unfinished. Music and voices are silent.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
