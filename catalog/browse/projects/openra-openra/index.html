Setup

  1. Choose the game you want to play

    The Windows portable ZIP contains three different players: RedAlert.exe, TiberianDawn.exe and Dune2000.exe. Extract the complete ZIP, then select the player for your chosen game and keep its folder as the working directory. The installer is an alternative; run it deliberately and select the installed player afterward. Server and utility tools are not game players.

  2. Finish content setup on first Play

    Choose Play explicitly to open that game’s own content screen. OpenRA can import your original media or offer its minimal content download; review that choice in OpenRA. No content installer or helper is started by discovery or by saving launch details.

  3. Choose the matching OpenRA game on Linux

    Choose the stable x86-64 AppImage named for Red Alert, Tiberian Dawn or Dune 2000. Make the complete AppImage executable and open it outside Portcove to finish that game’s content setup. Each AppImage starts its own game by default. Keep server and utility commands for their separate tasks.

  4. Use the OpenRA Windows ARM64 playtest

    Choose the official ARM64 Windows portable ZIP from a playtest release. Extract the whole ZIP and open RedAlert.exe, TiberianDawn.exe or Dune2000.exe from its own folder. The ARM64 package is a preview. Use the same OpenRA release as the other players if you play online.

  5. Use the OpenRA Flatpak on Linux

    Install net.openra.OpenRA from Flathub on Linux x64 or ARM64. Use the desktop launcher for Red Alert, Tiberian Dawn or Dune 2000 and finish content setup in that player. Back up the game’s Flatpak app data separately and update it through Flatpak. Use the official x64 AppImage if you need to switch between official and community mods outside the Flatpak sandbox.

  6. Choose the matching OpenRA application on Mac

    The stable upstream DMG contains a separate Red Alert, Tiberian Dawn and Dune 2000 application. The reviewed launchers include Intel and Apple Silicon. Copy the complete app you want out of the DMG and open it to finish content setup. Open that app directly to play and preserve its actual OpenRA profile.

Setup notes
Keep your upstream profile and update route
OpenRA or you provide updates. Preserve the actual profile: normally %APPDATA%\OpenRA on Windows, ~/.config/openra on Linux, or ~/Library/Application Support/OpenRA on macOS. A local Support folder, an older profile or a custom override can change it. Keep settings, saves, maps, mods and content together, and retain a working copy when changing versions. These external files are outside Portcove backup and rollback.
