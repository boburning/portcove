Setup

  1. Choose your edition of Atlantis

    In Atlantis Native, add your own Atlantis: The Lost Tales edition with matching atlantis.exe and BIGCD1.BIG through BIGCD4.BIG. Keep the complete original folder. Use the upstream edition table for the exact language and build.

  2. Start the Linux desktop player

    Extract the matching v0.6.0 Linux tar.gz and open atlantis-native. Both the x86-64 and ARM64 builds need glibc 2.35 or later and ALSA. On Windows 11 ARM, choose the Windows ARM64 ZIP and keep its folder together. Maintain the ARM builds through upstream.

Setup notes
Open the matching Mac app
Choose the Intel or Apple silicon v0.6.0 DMG, copy Atlantis Native.app out of it and open it on macOS 11 or later. Keep ~/Library/Application Support/atlantis-native. On Linux the profile normally uses $XDG_DATA_HOME/atlantis-native or ~/.local/share/atlantis-native.
Set up the PortMaster edition
On a compatible ARM64 PortMaster device, unpack the v0.6.0 PortMaster ZIP into ports and put your original first-game files in ports/atlantisnative/gamedata. Use the firmware menu to launch it. Keep the port folder, its saves directory and any ATLANTIS_SAVES override. This build needs the firmware SDL2 and glibc 2.31 or later.

Limitations and evidence

  • Preserve the original data and whole effective profile, including games.txt, settings and the edition-specific checkpoint directory. The player shares this profile between copies on the same account.
