Setup

  1. Supply your matching original

    For the managed Windows route, choose your own exact GoldenEye 007 NTSC-U USA big-endian .z64 original. Portcove verifies its declared identity and copies it to data/ge007.ntsc-final.z64 beside the player. PAL, Japanese, byte-swapped and modified images are not accepted by this contract. No BIOS or separate installer is required.

  2. Choose Play for first-run preparation

    Keep the complete official Windows ZIP together. Play starts ge007.x86_64.exe, which invokes its bundled frozen converter when generated sidecars are missing. Portcove does not run a discovered helper during download or setup, and does not certify successful upstream conversion or gameplay. If conversion fails, follow the selected upstream troubleshooting instructions and keep your original and saves recoverable.

  3. Use an existing copy on Linux or Windows

    For a copy you already have, keep the complete v0.5.0 folder and place your own matching USA NTSC-U big-endian ROM at data/ge007.ntsc-final.z64. Choose ge007.x86_64.exe on Windows or ge007.x86_64 on Linux, with its containing folder as the start folder. The Linux x64 kit uses glibc 2.29 or newer, system zlib and OpenGL, and its adjacent SDL2 library. Save your details in Portcove, then choose Play.

Setup notes
Preserve your actual data before changing copies
For an externally owned copy, preserve data/ge007.ntsc-final.z64, data/ge007.eep, data/ge007.ini, emulator-import backups and ppm screenshots, plus any selected custom locations. The upstream importer has 99 backup names and can overwrite its last slot when full. Keep generated sidecars and shader lists with their producing runtime; optional source/Python tools are separate deliberate actions. You or upstream provide external updates. Portcove registration does not update, back up, roll back or delete those external files.

Limitations and evidence

  • The upstream importer has 99 backup names and can overwrite its last slot when full.
  • On Linux v0.5.0, the in-game settings restart starts a new process that Portcove cannot track. Quit the game and choose Play again to keep the whole session visible in Portcove. If you already used the restart, close the new game window before changing the runtime or copying its data.
