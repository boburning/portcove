Setup

  1. Prepare your Windows copy

    Keep your complete permitted Windows x64 copy in a writable folder. Open Wetrix.exe and select your own 8 MiB, big-endian Wetrix USA revision 0 .z64 (SHA-256 790f36a3ddd64e4dcd08c479b0b66f9080d0191473e92fa895f53d15706167bb). The game verifies and imports it to Assets/data.z64.

  2. Select the prepared player

    Select Wetrix.exe and use its containing folder as the working directory. Leave arguments empty. Keep the complete independently permitted runtime and finish the title-specific preparation before Play.

Registration ownership and removal
Portcove records the selected executable, working folder and literal arguments after review. Play is separate and only the selected process is supervised. You and upstream own replacement and updates; relink changed executable bytes after reviewing them. Removing this registration preserves every external file. Managed installation, backup, rollback, migration and deletion are not offered for this external copy.
Player-data ownership
You and upstream own the external copy's data: Assets/data.z64, config/user/ and saves/controller1.mpk in the game folder. Back up these locations separately; registration does not migrate or certify their compatibility.
