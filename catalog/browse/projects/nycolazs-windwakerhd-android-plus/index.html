The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD (Wind Waker HD Recompiled [Android] - Plus)

A maintained Android Plus Wind Waker HD fork adding Brazilian Portuguese, localization and performance changes over the Enhanced and original Android ports. The APK prepares and statically compiles your own Wii U USA/Europe/Japan data on-device for ARM64/Vulkan. Keep this fork’s package, signing/update key and launcher together; parent packages are not interchangeable. Source builds need SDK 36, NDK 27.2 and JDK 17. Desktop support and independent campaign/device coverage are unconfirmed. Exact accepted game revision/hashes and BIOS/firmware requirements are unconfirmed. The project’s launcher manages replacement; Portcove has not confirmed setup or gameplay.
