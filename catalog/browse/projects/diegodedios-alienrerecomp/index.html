Setup

  1. Use your existing upstream player

    For an existing copy, select AlienResurrection.exe on Windows or AlienResurrection on Linux with its containing folder.

  2. Choose your original disc on Play

    Use your own USA PlayStation Alien Resurrection disc, SLUS-00633. The game launcher accepts its .cue, a .bin track or the containing folder and checks the edition.

  3. Set up the universal Mac app

    On Intel or Apple Silicon with macOS 11 or later, extract the upstream macOS ZIP with the system archive tool and keep the complete app. If downloading through Portcove, clear “Extract the archive after downloading”. Follow upstream’s first-launch instructions. Use your own supported USA disc. Preserve the actual AlienResurrection profile under Library/Application Support or your custom save directory.

Limitations and evidence

  • Existing external copies and their profiles stay upstream/player-owned. Runtime rollback does not rewind saves or guarantee older-version compatibility.
  • Make an in-game memory-card save before updating, switching versions or rolling back. Quick saves are tied to the port version and have one slot. Keep the working runtime if your latest progress exists only in its quick save.
