Setup

  1. Check the required game files

    Use your own Xbox 360 F.E.A.R. Files, title ID 565507E8, with default.xex and Game/ under port-files/assets/. Check docs/SETUP.md for the required region, revision and hash before preparing files. Base F.E.A.R., title ID 565507D9, is a different game. The optional Files PC texture pack requires your own corresponding PC files.

  2. Follow upstream setup

    For source-only version 0.1.0, prepare the patched SDK with tools/setup-sdk.ps1, then locally compile this title using tools/build-nx.ps1 -Project port-files. tools/launch.ps1 -Collection can select it after independent preparation. No public compiled game/player archive is offered by the README; keep your own disc data local under port-files/assets/.

Limitations and evidence

  • Upstream reports gameplay and 4K/80 FPS with an optional texture pack made from your own PC files, plus a right-stick fix. Smoke/effects, optimization and complete playthroughs of both expansion campaigns remain unfinished or unconfirmed. Base-game fixes do not necessarily apply. Native Linux is untested, Steam Deck uses experimental Proton and Android ARM64 work has not started. Portcove has not confirmed runtime behavior or gameplay.
  • Portcove has not confirmed setup, gameplay, saves or updates for this experimental port.
