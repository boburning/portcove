Setup

  1. Keep a permitted complete player

    Keep Brawl Recompiled.exe, engine and Disc together in the Windows release folder. On Linux use its separate Brawl Recompiled launcher and complete Linux package. Register the launcher with its folder as the working directory, save launch details, then choose Play separately. A prebuilt release needs no compiler; follow the upstream requirements for your platform.

  2. Choose your own supported disc on first Play

    Use the launcher's Play page to choose your own USA RSBE01 revision 1 Brawl ISO, CISO or WBFS. It validates the disc and extracts Disc/DATA, about 7 GB. A matching Dolphin-extracted Disc/DATA folder is another upstream route. Keep the original image recoverable; no original disc or automatic helper execution is supplied by Portcove. Reuse a valid remembered disc choice.

Setup notes
Keep saves, SD content and mods together
Close both game and launcher before copying data or replacing the runtime. Back up the complete engine/UserData tree in packaged releases, including Config.toml, NAND saves, SD and mods, plus any sd.raw card and configured custom disc, NAND or card roots. Development builds can instead use Play/UserData; use the folder opened by your actual launcher. Keep Disc and your original image separately. SD/card writes are copied back when the game exits, so wait for a clean exit. Use the upstream launcher for save import, controls and mod selection.
Update with a recoverable working copy
You or upstream own runtime updates and backups. Keep the current working copy and full data backup, prepare a new permitted version separately and verify your own progress before removing the old copy. Do not replace your data with a clean package. Portcove registration does not manage Update, Backup or Rollback; removing it leaves every external file untouched.
