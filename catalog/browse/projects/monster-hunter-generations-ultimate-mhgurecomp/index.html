Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate (MHGURecomp)

Upstream publishes a Windows x86-64 ZIP, a native Linux x86-64 tarball and an experimental Android ARM64 APK. Setup uses your own Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate 1.4.0 ExeFS and RomFS. The desktop releases include a separate upstream updater. Desktop needs AVX2 and Vulkan 1.2. Linux also needs glibc 2.39 or later. Android requires Android 10 or later, Vulkan 1.1 and a controller.
