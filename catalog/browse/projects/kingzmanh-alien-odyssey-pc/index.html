Setup

  1. Install from your own disc

    Open the publisher’s setup and select your original CD, ISO or BIN/CUE image, or a folder containing the extracted disc. Choose a new destination with about 500 MB available. Setup copies the disc and creates a native odyssey.exe entry point. Keep your original separately.

  2. Preserve saves and settings before removal

    Close the game and keep game/hd/SAVEGAME, game/hd/GAME.CFG and odyssey_pc.ini beside the player. The upstream uninstaller removes the installed game tree and its saves. Keep a separate backup before replacing or uninstalling it.

Limitations and evidence

  • The original’s sections run within the installed odyssey.exe player. The publisher’s setup and uninstaller own replacement and removal.
