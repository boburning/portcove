Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (Racing-8-DX-Recomp)

Racing-8-DX-Recomp builds a personal Mario Kart 8 Deluxe AppImage on Linux from your own game and 3.0.4 update. The project reports races and four-player play on AMD hardware. Its v0.1.0 tag contains no downloadable player.
