The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom (MacNativeGames)

An experimental Tears of the Kingdom Apple ARM64 rewrite with shared Horizon/NVN-to-Metal services and a local builder. Mac profiles cover 1.0.0, 1.1.2, 1.2.1 and 1.4.3, with limited performance/rendering/audio reports. iPhone/iPad 1.4.3 was played; other iOS versions are untested. A contributor visionOS demonstration is separate from current support, and no full playthrough is established. Apple Silicon Mac needs macOS 26+/Metal 4; iOS/iPadOS needs 26+ and iPhone 14+. Python 3.14 build_app.py builds a local app or signed/sideloaded IPA; no release package exists. Back up profiles before rebuilding/updating; rollback does not establish backward save compatibility. Supply your own 1.0.0 title 0100F2C0115B6000 base XCI/NSP and prod.keys, with optional cumulative 1.1.2/1.2.1/1.4.3 NSP/NSZ update. NSPs need a common ticket unless rights-free, plus your own firmware or six shared BFTTF fonts, potentially from the XCI update partition. DLC, other combined packages, compressed base games and personalized tickets are unsupported. Portcove offers no setup or launch integration and has not tested gameplay.
