Setup

  1. Finish the game’s setup

    On first Play, select your own North American v1.0 Donkey Kong Country 2 ROM in the launcher. It remembers that external path without copying the ROM. DKC2Recomp.exe is the upstream default; the bundled DKC2RecompSDL.exe remains an alternative through external registration. Character packs and MSU-1 audio are optional upstream-guided imports.

  2. Set up the Apple Silicon app

    Use the upstream Apple Silicon ZIP on macOS 26 or later. Extract the complete app outside your Portcove library and follow upstream’s first-launch instructions for the unnotarized app. Select your own North American v1.0 ROM. Preserve the Flat2VR/DKC2Recomp profile under Library/Application Support.
