The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — Switch edition (SavageWindRecomp)

SavageWindRecomp builds the Switch edition of Breath of the Wild on Windows or Linux from your own extracted 1.9.0 game. The project reports shrines and the Great Plateau. Later progress, controllers and gyro remain unconfirmed. DLC is unsupported.

Setup

  1. Check your original game files

    Prepare the 1.9.0 ExeFS with main, main.npdm, rtld, sdk and subsdk0, plus the complete merged RomFS. The launcher does not decrypt a game file or supply keys, firmware or extraction tools.

  2. Follow the project’s setup

    Use the Alpha 3 ZIP or AppImage launcher on Windows or Linux x64 with SSE4.2 and Vulkan 1.3. The launcher builds from your files locally. AVX2 and FMA can improve speed.

Setup notes
Version and platform notes
The Linux aarch64 build has not been tested on ARM hardware. Distant-map rendering remains incomplete. The project does not support version 1.8.2.
