Setup

  1. Prepare your Windows copy

    Use Windows x64 with OpenGL 4.5 support. Extract the Windows ZIP into a writable folder. Open FullRumble.exe and choose the disc image from your own USA or Canada PlayStation release (SLUS-01068). The setup accepts CUE/BIN, a supported standalone BIN, or ISO. Wait for the game to finish its local build before adding this copy to Portcove.

  2. Select the prepared player

    Select FullRumble.exe and use its containing folder as the working directory. Leave arguments empty. Keep the complete independently permitted runtime and finish the title-specific preparation before Play.

Setup notes
Registration ownership and removal
Portcove records the selected executable, working folder and literal arguments after review. Play is separate and only the selected process is supervised. You and upstream own replacement and updates; relink changed executable bytes after reviewing them. Removing this registration preserves every external file. Managed installation, backup, rollback, migration and deletion are not offered for this external copy.
Player-data ownership
You and upstream own the external copy's data: carda.sav, cardb.sav, settings.json and interface.ini in the working folder; %APPDATA%/FullRumble for setup settings and the generated library; disc/ beside the program, or %LOCALAPPDATA%/FullRumble/disc if the program folder is not writable. Back up these locations separately; registration does not migrate or certify their compatibility.

Limitations and evidence

  • Keep your original disc image and the memory-card files. Use the game’s own updater outside a Portcove Play session, then review the changed program in Portcove.
