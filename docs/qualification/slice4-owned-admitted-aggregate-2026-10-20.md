# Admitted aggregate preparation and recovery

The fixed combined `building` operation remains the `admission` reader. The
selected preparation reallocates the existing 19 job-minutes to one nine-minute
admission/assembly reader and one ten-minute observer; the separate attester
keeps its existing two minutes. These are finite proposed limits, not measured
hosted timing or a grant to execute. The caller supplies one original job
deadline across admission, Git, assembly, upload, readback and predicate creation.
Every fresh control round still needs the same actual observer; expiry holds.

`runFixedAdmittedAggregateConfiguration` authenticates actual tools and current
compiler/native/SPDX/public inputs in the existing protected admission job. It
advances qualifying to building once, or independently reacquires an exact
building reservation with retained stage/run/counter evidence. It creates no
cross-job private admission receipt. Legacy plain-ref assembler verification
remains a separate API and profile.

The named custody acquisition may also recheck publishing/promoting reservations
with their retained exact stage history. Its private proof is readback-only:
the ordinary building getter and the aggregate upload transition both refuse it.
It can recover the same settled aggregate artifact and prepare current-custodian
attestation output, but cannot advance phase, assemble packages or upload again.
Reusing payload contexts and role envelopes still requires their own actual
authenticated receivers; aggregate custody cannot manufacture those locators.

Ordinary GH/Git children in this actual callable inherit a captured runtime
whitelist, private empty CLI home and the explicit ordinary job read token.
Journal, signing/App, OIDC runner bearer and runtime-injection variables are
excluded. The parent retains its separately authenticated journal transport;
the environment scope does not establish provider permissions or job authority.
Existing callers outside this scope keep their existing behavior.

Before an aggregate upload, the existing coordinator journal binds its assembly
digest and sorted bounded output-file inventory into the exclusive pending
operation. Only the fixed admitted assembler can request this effect. The
admission-only transport event invokes the maintained upload Action; its ACK is
only a locator. Complete unique artifact inventory, source identities, archive
digest and every maintained package/checksum member must agree before the
existing journal records completion. Missing or partial readback stays unknown.

A resumed pending upload requires positive terminal observation of its original
exact producer before and after GET-only readback. A completed upload retained
in compact journal history is hydrated from its original immutable checkpoint.
Neither path rebuilds or uploads again. The current independently authenticated
custodian may prepare a new aggregate attestation predicate, preserving the
original artifact, producer/job/run, pending operation and expectation digest
separately from its own current identity. The fixed admitted recovery consumer
binds both; it does not relabel an old failed job as successful. Actual Sigstore
verification remains the existing independent downstream prerequisite.

Local fixtures use disposable identity keys, ordinary temporary files and
injected provider/verifier responses. They establish contract and byte-flow
behavior only. They do not establish real provider identity, observer timing,
signing, native acceptance, publication or initial setup authority. No protected
operation was executed to prepare this change.
