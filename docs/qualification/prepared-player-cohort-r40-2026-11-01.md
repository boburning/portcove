The frozen batch contains 36 individually traced source/setup and prepared-player guides. The [structured evidence packet](prepared-player-cohort-r40-2026-11-01.json) binds authored pre/postimages, canonical specifications, selected public source and inspected official packages. Detailed acceptance stays on each owner.

Preparation and replacement remain upstream-owned. Registration specifies the normal executable, working directory and arguments; Play is a separate request. Removing registration preserves external runtimes, originals and data. Managed installation, update, rollback, backup and deletion remain unavailable. These are Source contract known proposals, not Supported/native/gameplay observations.
