| Previous consolidated setup                                                | Approved direction                                                                                                                               |

| Local native qualification                                                 | Disposable standard hosted Windows/Ubuntu, only after actual installed-client continuity and resource fit pass                                   |

Single-owner custody deliberately loses independent quorum compromise resilience. An unlocked vault compromise can defeat Root trust. Encryption on connected storage is not air-gapped custody. Root stays outside Actions and the reviewer; its unchanged maximum 365-day expiry still requires exceptional owner maintenance. Online Targets adds authority to the protected signer and must be explicitly scoped. Ordinary releases and Targets renewal need zero recurring owner approvals, passwords or elevation.

The client/tool change is not a setting: Core's compiled catalog authority requires three Root keys and threshold two, and release-tools validates/builds that quorum. Implement a fixed reviewed initial profile, preserve existing anchor validation, and reconcile any actual published Root/rotation history before changing trust. Never let candidate input choose a weaker profile. Freeze the new capable client before #246 initial absence; do not change it during definition, artifact progression and correction.
