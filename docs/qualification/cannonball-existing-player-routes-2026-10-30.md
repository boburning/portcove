## Why the existing listing-based route remains the correct contract

The unchanged authoring guard refuses a restricted listing alongside a strict
integration. Core's standalone strict-catalog reader synthesizes descriptive
listings from definitions without the listing-only download restriction. Adding
a definition could therefore lose the condition during standalone delivery.

The already-delivered listing-based registration instead retains the **whole
restricted listing** in `portcove-user-selected-listing` format 1, with the
existing reader fence. It supplies the useful user-selected operations without
adding a managed definition. The complete retained listing, including the
restriction and guide, is checked after restart. No new authority, schema,
protocol field or relaxed authoring policy is introduced. A definition count is
not a useful-route or supported-operation count.
